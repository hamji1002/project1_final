As I move closer, separation begins.

Foreground and background stop agreeing.

Some elements pull forward while others recede.

I start to notice where my attention lingers

Holding Patterns
Glance
briefly seen
Held
felt before naming
Kept
returned to
Revisited
seen again, differently
These describe how attention stayed, not what was photographed.

Distance begins to register.

Not as space, but as separation.

Looking no longer feels neutral.

Attention shifts slightly—

not enough to commit,

but enough to notice that some things pull harder than others.

This layer operates at an in-between distance.
