At this distance, surface begins to resit.

Details assert themselves unevenly.

I become aware of standing inside the frame

Holding Patterns
Glance
briefly seen
Held
felt before naming
Kept
returned to
Revisited
seen again, differently
These describe how attention stayed, not what was photographed.

Distance compresses.

What was once evenly distributed starts to compete.

Edges do not simply frame anymore;

they press inward.

Attention is no longer free to drift.

It gathers unevenly,

drawn by interruption, contrast, resistance.

Reading becomes selective.

Some parts are skipped without permission.

Others demand repeated passes.

At this distance, meaning does not arrive all at once.

It assembles through friction.

This layer operates indoors.
