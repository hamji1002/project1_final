At this proximity, clarify fragments.

Texture overtakes form.

I stop reading the whole.

My attention moves in peices, following contact rather than structure

Holding Patterns
Glance
briefly seen
Held
felt before naming
Kept
returned to
Revisited
seen again, differently
These describe how attention stayed, not what was photographed.

Distance stops functioning as an organizing principle.

Attention breaks into local decisions.

It moves by contact rather than structure or hierarchy.

Reading becomes fragmented.

Meaning is no longer inferred from the whole,

because the whole cannot be held at once.

Instead, significance accumulates in pieces:

partial impressions, repeated surfaces.

At this proximity, clarity does not sharpen.

What remains is not understanding,

but density—

a layered presence that resists being summarized.

This layer operates with surface.
